# Getting started

Flow tracks the gear, the projects, and the people at a production company —
built by one that needed exactly that, then shaped since by what other
production companies asked for.

This section is a short, ordered setup path: five pages, done in order, and
you'll have your organization live, your gear loaded, your crew invited, and
a first project out the door. Work through it with a trial org to see how
Flow behaves, or use your own gear and clients from the start — either way
you finish with a real, working setup. Everything else in this guide is
reference you dip into once you're up and running.

## The setup path

1. **[Create your organization](./create-your-organization)** — your
   company's walled-off workspace, set up first.
2. **[Set up your catalogue](./set-up-your-catalogue)** — categories,
   models, assets, bulk stock, and kits, loaded in the order they build on
   each other.
3. **[Invite your team](./invite-your-team)** — members who sign in, crew
   who get scheduled onto jobs.
4. **[Your first project](./your-first-project)** — one lap of the track:
   create a job, quote it, send gear out, get it back.
5. **[Quick tour](./quick-tour)** — the sidebar, search, and the shortcuts,
   once the basics are in.
