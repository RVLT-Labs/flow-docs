# How your catalogue works

Flow tracks gear in three layers, and once the distinction clicks, the whole
app makes sense: a **model** is the spec ("Shure SM58" — rates, weight,
photo), an **asset** is one physical serialised unit of it (with its own tag
and history), and **bulk stock** is a counted quantity of things too cheap
to serialise (XLR leads, sandbags).
