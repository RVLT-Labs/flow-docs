# Versions, locks and unlocking

Once a job is confirmed, its numbers are a promise to the client, so Flow
starts protecting them. This page explains the locks — and the deliberate,
audited ways to change things anyway.

## What this page will cover

- The lock tiers in plain language: open → finance-locked (confirmed) →
  justify-to-edit (on site) → hard-locked (completed)
- What "finance-locked" actually blocks, and why new gear added to a
  confirmed job comes in at $0
- Unlock sessions: opening one with a written reason, making your changes,
  then Save & relock or Discard
- Versions: the snapshot taken at every confirmation and unlock, and the
  diff view that shows what changed between them
- Who can unlock, and what the audit trail records
