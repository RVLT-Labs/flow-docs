# How projects work

A project is a job: a client, dates, a gear list, crew, tasks, and the
paperwork that goes with all of it. Everything else in Flow exists to serve
one. This overview explains a project's anatomy and — the part that trips
everyone up once — its lifecycle: the further a job progresses, the more of
it locks down on purpose.

## What this overview will cover

- The anatomy of a project: header (client, dates, locations), equipment
  (line items and groups), services and crew, tasks, documents, activity
- The status lifecycle, start to finish, and what each status means
- What each stage locks: prices freeze at confirmation, structural edits
  need a written reason once the job is on site, and a completed job is
  read-only — with pointers to
  [Versions and unlocking](/docs/projects/versions-and-unlocking)
- Project numbering — where those job codes come from
- The project as the money's home: quoted, confirmed, invoiced totals
