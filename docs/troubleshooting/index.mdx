# Fix a problem

Flow said no, something looks wrong, or a number isn't what you expected.
These pages are titled by symptom — find yours.

## Planned articles

**Documents**

- Why can't I regenerate this quote/invoice PDF? _(sent documents are
  stored bytes, on purpose — make a new version instead)_

- Why did this quote expire on its own?

**Availability and warehouse**

- Why is gear showing as overbooked?
- Why does a kit show unavailable when its parts look free?
- Why won't the returns station accept this scan? _(over-return, or the
  unit isn't out)_
- Why can't I deploy this asset? _(maintenance, T&T due, or another job)_

**Access**

- Why can't I see costs and margins?
- Why can't I find a project I know exists? _(templates are excluded, or
  role visibility)_
