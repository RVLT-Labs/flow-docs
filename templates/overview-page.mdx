# How X works

Open with the one mental model the section depends on, in two or three
sentences. (Example: the model / asset / bulk stock distinction; why sent
documents freeze.) If the reader gets this paragraph, every task page in
the section becomes obvious.

## The parts

Explain the anatomy: what the pieces are and how they relate. Use a table
when the facts are enumerable, prose when they need connecting. No task
steps here — link to the task page instead.

## The lifecycle (if the thing has one)

Statuses, transitions, and what each stage means for the reader — what they
can still change, what's now locked, who else can see what.

:::note
Callouts on an overview page are for cross-cutting truths ("this applies
everywhere in Flow"), not task-level tips.
:::
